# 03 — User research · Hygisense

> Status (26 Nov 2026): **no interviews were run — a team decision.** Pharmacists already lose more than 10 h a week on shortages (PGEU survey), and the team chose not to take their time for a course project. The guide below stays ready for the pilot. **We did not invent any result.**
>
> **What we use instead (secondary sources, cited in 01_problem_statement.md):** the PGEU 2024-25 survey of European community pharmacists (time spent, +4 h vs 2022), the Ordre des pharmaciens figures on DP-Ruptures, and the DREES/ANSM study on shortages declared by manufacturers (March 2025).
>
> **What this leaves untested — our riskiest user assumptions:** (1) pharmacists tick BUY lines, (2) they accept some useless buy-aheads, (3) the screen fits their morning. The pilot measures all three in shadow mode and in the A/B (lines ticked, refused, edited — 06_07 §1).

## Interview guide — 20 minutes, no pitch before the end

**Warm-up (2 min)** — What is your role? How many orders do you place a day?

**The last time (10 min) — facts, not opinions**
1. Tell me about the **last** drug you could not get. When did you find out? Where were you?
2. What did you do next — step by step? Which websites, which calls?
3. How long did it take, roughly? Who else was involved?
4. Did a patient leave without their treatment? What happened?
5. Did you know it was coming? If yes, how — a lab notice, DP-Ruptures, a colleague?
6. What do you do with DP-Ruptures today? When was the last time you opened it?

**Numbers we need for 02_sizing.xlsx (3 min)**
7. In a normal week, how much time goes into shortages? (checks the 10 h/week)
8. Have you ever bought extra stock because you felt a shortage coming? What made you do it, how much did you buy, and was it right? (checks the buy-ahead logic and the cap)
8b. When you knew a week ahead, how long did it take compared to a surprise? (checks the 60 % time saved)
9. In a normal month, how many patients do you turn away because a drug is missing? How many chronic patients have you lost for good after a shortage? (checks branches B and C of the value — the biggest numbers)
9b. Do you already pay for any subscription on top of your pharmacy software? How much? (checks the €99/month)

**Close (5 min)** — only now, show the morning list in one sentence.
10. "If you had had this list last time, what would you have done differently?" — not "would you use it?"

**Never ask:** "Would you use this?", "Is this a good idea?", "How much would you pay?" (everybody says yes to be nice).

## Prototype test — one task, 20 minutes

**Task:** "It is 8:30, you open the pharmacy. Using this screen, decide what you will buy ahead today, and tell us one recommendation you would refuse and why."
Note where they hesitate, what they expected, what they ignored. Don't help.

**Pass (the desirability gate):** 4 of 5 finish without help · they tell us what they did last time, not what they would do · one says "I want this before…".
